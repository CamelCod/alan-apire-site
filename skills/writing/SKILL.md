# Alanpire writing

Draft 0.1. Read `../../AUDIT.md` and `../../REFERENCES.md` first.

## Purpose

Write technically precise, commercially clear copy without overstating licensing, project ownership or outcomes.

## Rules

- Put the deliverable before abstract claims of expertise.
- Keep legal entity name separate from the public brand.
- Distinguish service areas from exact licensed activities.
- Attribute prior work to practitioners and the entity that performed it.
- Preserve engineering values and standards across EN/AR/RU.
- Never fabricate people, UAE projects, client logos, endorsements or savings.
- Use neutral Modern Standard Arabic and direct professional Russian, not literal English translations.

## Example

Before: “Four Licensed Service Areas”.

After: “Technical support for industrial projects”.

Reason: describes the commercial offer without implying four separately licensed activities.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has evidence or is marked for client verification.
- [ ] Local consultant responsibilities remain clear.
- [ ] CTAs describe the action, not “Submit”.
- [ ] AR/RU convey the same scope and limitations as English.
- [ ] Metadata and on-page brand agree.
