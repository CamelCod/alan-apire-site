# District Heating Network Reconstruction, Vektor Research Centre

Location: Koltsovo, Novosibirsk Oblast, Russia. Second-stage reconstruction.

## Facts
- Upgrade: 2 x DN400 to 2 x DN500, 1,704 m, Heat Node 1 to Node 48
- Pipe: 530 x 9 mm, steel 09G2S, above ground on existing estacade
- Medium: network water. Strength design basis 150 C and 2.0 MPa
- Category IVe, Rostechnadzor registration required
- Design life 30 years, max 3,000 cold starts

## Summary
Alan Apire's team supported documentation for the reconstruction of a 1,704 m district-heating main. The twin-pipe DN400 main was upgraded to DN500 on the existing elevated supports. Scope covered fire safety, safe operation, drainage, air release, insulated factory-ready pipe sections, service platforms and strengthening of the estacade. [CONFIRM ACTUAL ROLE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

## Technical points
- Welds: longitudinal 100% NDT (UT + RT); circumferential at least 3% UT for pipe up to 465 mm, more if defects found.
- Hydraulic test: 1.25 x working pressure, not below 1.0 MPa. Annual external inspection and test.
- Inspections: every 2 weeks in heating season, monthly off-season, heat nodes monthly.
- Minimum gradient 0.002. Planned emptying of each pipeline within 4 hours, once a year.
- Protective zone 3 m each side of estacade. Thermal expansion by route bends and U-compensators.
- Site: frost-heave soils, perched groundwater, flood risk, frost depth 183 cm, snow zone III, wind zone III.
- Winter: install above 0 C where practical; avoid outdoor pipe handling below -15 C; welding below -10 C only in heated enclosures at 0 C or more.

## Fire safety
The medium is water, so the heat main is not explosive or fire-hazardous. No automatic fire suppression, alarm, evacuation warning or smoke control is required for the main. The main fire risk is during construction: PPU insulation and polyethylene are combustible. Controls: separate storage, no hot work within 2 m of insulated pipe storage, extinguishers, training, hot-work control, emergency procedures.
