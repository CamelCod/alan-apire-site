# 10 MW Municipal Boiler House, Karpinsk

Location: Karpinsk, Sverdlovsk Region, Russia
Client: MKU Municipal Economy Management

## Facts
- Installed capacity 10.5 MW; net output 10.132 MW
- 3 x RSD 3500 hot-water boilers; two gas/diesel burners and one gas burner
- 1,000 L emergency diesel tank; plate heat exchangers, network pumps, water softening
- Network flow up to 408.5 m3/h
- Plot 4,000 m2 (3,144 m2 in design boundary); boiler building 371.04 m2
- Two 55 m3 underground firewater tanks; diesel unloading pad; multi-flue chimney
- Boiler house cost RUB 53.01 million; consolidated budget RUB 91.32 million incl. 22% VAT (Q1 2026 prices)
- Planned construction: 7 months
- Positive state expertise conclusion 66-1-1-3-028194-2026, 18 June 2026
