Build on the existing Astro repo. Keep /en, /ar, /ru separate. Write each language natively. Do not machine-translate.

## Positioning
Headline: Industrial MEP, Engineering and Project Delivery Support
Subheadline: We help industrial and infrastructure clients manage technical scope, contractors, drawings, schedules, and project handover, from concept to completion.
Primary CTA: Discuss Your Project on WhatsApp. Secondary CTA: Request a Technical Review.

## Pages
Home, Services (4), Sectors (district heating, energy and utilities, water and infrastructure, oil and gas), Case studies (Vektor, Karpinsk), About, Contact, Privacy, Terms. Do not create empty pages.

## Services (in this order)
1. Industrial MEP and Engineering Design: MEP review, calculations, equipment layouts, specifications.
2. Project Management and Site Coordination: schedules, contractors, reporting, risks, handover.
3. Technical Drawings and Documentation: concept layouts, equipment arrangements, as-built review.
4. Technical and Commercial Advisory: feasibility, BOQ, cost review, vendor evaluation, procurement.

## Copy rules
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No buzzwords.
- State client type, place, scope, role and result.
- Use supported, reviewed, prepared, coordinated, managed. Do not say designed or delivered unless Alan Apire was the designer of record or contractor.
- No claims such as zero losses, guaranteed approval, megaproject discipline.
- Remove: engineering substance not theoretical advice; analytical rigour; verified delivery experience (without evidence).
- No confidential client names, drawings or costs without permission.
- Confirm licensed activities in DIEZ licence and match wording (project management, consultancy, technical drawings) before claiming engineering design.

## Technical
- hreflang for en, ar, ru. RTL for Arabic.
- Unique title and meta description per page.
- JSON-LD ProfessionalService with email, phone, area served, services.
- sitemap, robots.txt, security headers (_headers), HTTPS, HSTS, CSP.
- WebP/AVIF images, lazy loading, mobile-first, accessible contrast and keyboard use.
- Analytics events: WhatsApp click, email click, form submit, case-study scroll.
- Intake form fields: name, company, country, sector, project stage, service needed, budget range, timeline, message, optional file.

## Design
Dark navy, steel grey, white, one accent colour. Real project visuals or neutral technical drawings. Sticky WhatsApp button on mobile.
