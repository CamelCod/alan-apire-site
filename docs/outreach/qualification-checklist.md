# Partner qualification — internal only

- Confirm the firm's legal identity, current licence category and authorised decision-maker through approved records.
- Confirm that the partner holds the client relationship and can appoint a sub-consultant under its contract.
- Define project type, location, stage, deliverables, input quality, review points and required timeline.
- Separate Alanpire technical input from partner professional decisions, stamping, submissions and NOCs.
- Confirm required UAE accreditation before accepting work that depends on it.
- Agree an NDA and secure exchange arrangement before receiving confidential documents. A checked request box is not an executed NDA.
- Identify project-required standards without promising blanket compliance.
- Verify actual capacity and responsibilities before promising dates or accepting a package.
- Use only approved, correctly attributed prior-team examples. Do not use excluded or commercially confidential documents.
- Record unresolved facts as `[TO CONFIRM]` in internal notes; do not publish them as experience or guarantees.
- Confirm the enquiry is received and assigned; a click, mail draft or provider receipt is not acceptance of the project.
