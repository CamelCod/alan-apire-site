# Reference research and adoption decisions

**26 November 2026.** Observations are source-specific. “Why it works” means a reasoned usability/design explanation, **not** proven uplift or a claim that a reference passes accessibility testing.

### Licence and originality policy

The two site skills are newly authored, site-specific instructions. No source skill, screenshot, photo, logo or substantial passage of third-party guidance is redistributed. Only general principles, attributed comparisons and source links are carried forward. For future copying, verify the **specific file's** licence at the pinned revision and preserve required notices; no licence or an ambiguous licence is not permission to copy.
