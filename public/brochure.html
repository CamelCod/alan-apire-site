Flagship Project

Twin DN500 High-Pressure Thermal Main Upgrade

FBRI SRC VB "Vector", Koltsovo, Novosibirsk Oblast, Russia
2×DN500
Transmission Mains
1.7 km
Main Route Length
20 bar
Design Pressure
87.5 Gcal/h
Thermal Capacity

Challenge: Reconstruction of an existing twin-pipe high-pressure thermal main from DN400 to DN500, with new connection to a central custody-transfer heat-metering node.

Scope: The 1,704 m corridor traverses an active industrial campus on elevated steel pipe-rack trestles (up to 6.49 m clearance). Engineering included thermal routing, hydraulic coordination, structural pipe-rack strengthening, U-loop expansion compensation, custody metering skid design, and full statutory approval package.

Result: State Expert Examination (Gosexpertiza) approved the complete design dossier. The upgraded network delivers zero unproductive losses, reduced pumping energy, and stable supply for both industrial processes and community needs.
