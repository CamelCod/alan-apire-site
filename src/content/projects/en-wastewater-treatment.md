The project aimed to protect health and support sanitary conditions through wastewater treatment.

## The solution described

The supplied project brief lists screens, grit chambers and primary settling tanks; aeration with activated sludge; neutralisation, coagulation and flocculation; ultraviolet disinfection; and sludge digestion and dewatering.
