## Project Context

The municipal operator required the rehabilitation of an aging water transmission corridor. The project scope required complete hydraulic redesign, pressure surge mitigation, and transition to high-density polyethylene piping.

## Fact Strip

- **Client Context**: Regional water utility authority
- **Location**: Municipal Infrastructure, Eurasia
- **Project Stage**: Detailed Engineering Design
- **Team Role**: Design Engineering Partner through OOO Energodiagnostika
- **Key Figures**: 8.4 km pipeline route, DN300 polyethylene piping, 2.4 bar operating pressure

## Engineering Contribution

- Executed steady-state hydraulic network modelling to establish operating pressure regimes and pump head requirements.
- Selected high-density polyethylene (PE 100 SDR 17) materials to eliminate corrosion and resist frost heave.
- Engineered sectional valve chambers with air release and wash-out drain nodes along the 8.4 km route.
- Produced trenching, bedding, and backfill specifications for cold-climate subsoil conditions.
- Prepared construction sequencing schedules to ensure municipal water supply continuity during phased tie-ins.

## Design Record

The engineering package met all statutory civil infrastructure requirements:
- **Design Standard**: SP 31.13330.2012 (Water Supply: Pipelines and Potable Networks)
- **Materials Compliance**: Certified PE 100 pressure piping standards
- **Deliverables**: Hydraulic calculation book, plan and profile drawings, and material take-offs
