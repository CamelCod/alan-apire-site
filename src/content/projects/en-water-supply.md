## The brief

An ageing water network served a Russian power station and a nearby settlement.
The customer needed a design for renewing trunk and local supply pipelines.

## The team's work

The engineering team prepared the technical re-equipment design.
The supplied project brief specifies replacing deteriorated metal pipe with 400 mm HDPE pipe.

## Design purpose

The design addressed water transport and replacement of ageing pipework.
This case does not claim measured savings, uninterrupted supply, water-quality certification or verified future capacity.
