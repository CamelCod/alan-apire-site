## Challenge: Capacity Upgrade on a Live Thermal Corridor

The FBRI State Research Center of Virology and Biotechnology "Vector" of Rospotrebnadzor required the reconstruction of an existing twin-pipe high-pressure thermal main in Koltsovo, Novosibirsk Oblast. The original DN400 corridor could no longer meet the thermal demand of the expanded research campus, and the operator needed a capacity upgrade that preserved supply continuity while integrating a new central custody-transfer heat-metering node.

The project demanded more than pipe replacement. It required hydraulic coordination with existing overhead pipe-rack structures, power supply to actuated valves, construction-corridor planning through an operating research facility, and authority-ready documentation acceptable to Russian state expertise review.

## Solution: DN500 Thermal Main with Integrated Metering and Automation

Alan Apire delivered the complete design-stage package for the upgraded thermal corridor:

**Thermal network design and hydraulic coordination** — Reconstructed twin transmission mains sized at 2 × DN500 over a main route length of 1,704 m, designed for a pressure basis of 20.0 bar and an approximate thermal capacity of 101.7 MWth. The design maintains hydraulic compatibility with existing overhead pipe-rack structures while eliminating bottlenecks.

**Custody-transfer metering interface** — Engineered the connection to a central custody-transfer heat-metering node, enabling accurate thermal energy accounting at the campus boundary. The interface includes 0.4 kV power tie-in and PLC/SCADA integration with telemetry for operational monitoring.

**Service platforms and supports** — Designed service platforms, anchor supports and drainage chambers along the route, with construction-corridor constraints mapped to protect ongoing research operations.

**External anti-corrosion protection** — Specified a two-layer mastic primer with mastic topcoat, with material quantities accounted for in the bill of materials.

**Authority-ready documentation** — Produced multi-volume design documentation, QRA input, environmental and site-clearance reports, and construction-corridor planning documents structured for state expertise submission.

## Results: Scalable Thermal Capacity with Full Traceability

The DN500 upgrade delivers a step-change in thermal distribution capacity while preserving the operational integrity of the research campus:

**Capacity uplift** — The corridor now supports the expanded thermal demand of the Vector campus without relying on temporary bypass or redundant generation.

**Measurement accuracy** — The custody-transfer node provides verified thermal energy data for billing, efficiency analysis and regulatory reporting.

**Operational visibility** — PLC/SCADA integration delivers real-time telemetry on flow, pressure and temperature, enabling predictive maintenance and rapid fault isolation.

**Regulatory compliance** — The design package was prepared to satisfy Russian state expertise requirements, including QRA input and environmental clearance documentation.

**Minimal disruption** — Construction-corridor planning and phased installation approach protected research activities throughout the upgrade.

This project demonstrates Alan Apire's ability to deliver complex thermal infrastructure upgrades on live, high-consequence sites — combining engineering depth, disciplined documentation and practical construction sequencing.
